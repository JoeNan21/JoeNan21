# Commercial thinking

The lens applied to every case, regardless of mode.

## The system view

```
Capture -> Qualify -> Advance -> Close -> Deliver -> Expand -> Retain -> Refer
```

A recommendation is evaluated on its effect on the **whole system**, not the
immediate stage. Advancing a deal that cannot be delivered is a loss recorded
later. Closing a customer that will not be retained is a loss recorded later.

## What the engine looks for in every case

| Signal | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| `pain_verified` | Is there a real, stated, costed problem? |
| `economic_buyer` | Who can actually approve spend? |
| `approval_chain` | Who else must say yes, and in what order? |
| `urgency_evidence` | Is there a dated, external reason to act now? |
| `cost_of_inaction` | What does doing nothing cost, quantified? |
| `unit_economics` | Does the money work at the individual transaction level? |
| `conversion_evidence` | Has attention previously converted to revenue here? |
| `opportunity_cost` | What is displaced by choosing this? |
| `reversibility` | If wrong, how expensive is the reversal? |
| `proof_evidence` | Has this been demonstrated at small scale first? |
| `referral_potential` | Does this multiply beyond itself? |
| `competition` | What is the alternative the counterparty is comparing against? |

Absence is informative. A missing `economic_buyer` is not neutral — it caps
confidence and triggers a red-team finding.

## Bottleneck bias

The engine prefers the smallest action that relieves the **binding constraint**
over the largest action that improves a non-binding one. Where a case does not
identify a constraint, that is recorded as a critical unknown.

## Anti-patterns the engine actively penalises

- **Activity mistaken for progress** — meetings held, posts published, emails
  sent, with no stage advancement.
- **Prestige mistaken for commercial value** — audience, brand name, title, or
  reach with no evidence of conversion.
- **Correlation mistaken for causation** — an outcome attributed to an action
  with no counterfactual.
- **Urgency invented rather than evidenced** — deadlines with no external anchor.
- **Volume mistaken for quality** — pipeline value with no qualification evidence.

Each maps to a red-team check in `cognition/red-team.md`.
